Arty, within reach

A direct way in.
On your device.

Use Arty in your browser, add its icon to your home screen, or access the Android beta if you have an invitation.

Already using Arty? Keep your existing workspace.

A new installation or signing in to the same account does not automatically transfer local conversations and files. Keep your existing application and its data.

The old domain, another browser or profile, the installed web version and the Android APK may have separate workspaces. Two Arty icons do not guarantee shared data. Restore and cross-device sync are not available.

Before using the installation menu, open Arty’s home page in your chosen browser. Do not install this guide page.

Installed web version · PWA

Android with Chrome

  1. In Chrome, visit tryarty.com.
  2. Open the three-dot menu and look for the installation option, sometimes under “Install and create shortcut”.
  3. Choose “Install”, then confirm in the browser’s dialog. Next, open the Arty icon.

Menu labels vary by Chrome version. If the option is missing, you can keep using Arty in the browser.

Installed web version · PWA

iPhone and iPad with Safari

  1. In Safari, visit tryarty.com.
  2. Open the page’s sharing options, directly or through the More menu. Look for “Add to Home Screen”, possibly under More.
  3. If offered, enable “Open as Web App”, then confirm the addition. Next, open the Arty icon on your home screen.

The layout varies by iOS or iPadOS version. If the sharing action is missing, check Apple’s instructions for your device.

Installed web version · PWA

Desktop with Chrome

  1. In Chrome, visit tryarty.com.
  2. Use the install icon near the address if it appears, or look for installation in Chrome’s sharing and saving menu.
  3. Confirm the installation offered by your browser, then open Arty from your applications.

Without an installation option, you can still use Arty in a tab. Other browsers may offer a different menu.

Native Android application · invitation only

APK beta with Firebase

This option is for invited testers. This guide does not provide a public APK download or a Google Play link.

  1. On Android, open your Firebase App Distribution invitation and accept it with a Google account.
  2. Find Arty in the tester portal using the account you used to accept the invitation. It may differ from your Arty account.
  3. Follow Firebase’s download and installation instructions. The App Tester application is optional.

If you have no invitation or cannot see Arty, contact support. Access is not automatic.

Installation, account and data are three different things.

My conversations or subscription seem to be missing

Check the site address, your browser or profile, and the account shown in Arty. Installation alone does not restore local data or VIP access. Do not uninstall your existing application or erase its data to try to recover them. Contact support with your device type and a description of the issue, without passwords or private content.

Do the web version and APK do exactly the same things?

No. The installed web version uses your browser; the APK is the native Android application. Some features differ: IMAP mail connections are native Android only. Installing one version does not convert the other or automatically share its local workspace.

Can I use Arty without an internet connection?

AI responses and Google services need internet access. Some previously loaded content may remain visible, but this does not guarantee full offline functionality. If this guide is unavailable offline, reconnect to the internet and reload it.

I cannot find the installation menu

Open tryarty.com directly in Chrome or Safari, outside another application’s built-in browser. Installation depends on your browser and device; it may also have been done already. You can use Arty in a tab without changing your existing installation.
